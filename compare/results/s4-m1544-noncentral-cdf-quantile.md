# S4-M1544 Noncentral Chi-Squared, Chi, and F CDF/Quantile

Date: 2026-11-15

## Change

Completed the noncentral-family diagnostics surface with free functions and
reusable-sampler methods:

- `noncentralChiSquaredCdf` / `noncentralChiSquaredQuantile` and
  `NoncentralChiSquared(T).cdf` / `.quantile`.
- `noncentralChiCdf` / `noncentralChiQuantile` and
  `NoncentralChi(T).cdf` / `.quantile` (delegates through `x²`).
- `noncentralFCdf` / `noncentralFQuantile` and
  `NoncentralF(T).cdf` / `.quantile`.

Noncentral chi-squared and noncentral F evaluate the Poisson(λ/2) mixture
around its mode: weights are generated by the exact recurrence in both
directions, the sum is renormalized by the accumulated weight, and terms stop
below `1e-19` relative weight. This is exact for `λ/2 ≤ 1e5` (a few thousand
terms). Above that threshold they use the Patnaik two-moment central
approximation:

- `χ'²(k, λ) ≈ c·χ²_h` with `c = (k+2λ)/(k+λ)` and `h = (k+λ)²/(k+2λ)`.
- `F'(d1, d2, λ) ≈ (c·h/d1)·F(h, d2)` through the same `c`/`h`.

Noncentral chi-squared with `k = 0` keeps the `e^{−λ/2}` atom at zero in both
the CDF and quantile. `NoncentralT` still has no CDF/quantile; it needs a
normal/chi-squared integral rather than a finite mixture and remains a
documented gap.

## Validation

Cross-checks:

- `λ = 0` matches `chiSquaredCdf` and `fisherFCdf` to `1e-12`.
- `χ'²(2, ν²)` matches `riceCdf(√x, ν, 1)` to `1e-6` (independent Marcum-Q
  implementation).
- Quantile round-trips for `p = 1e-6, 0.3, 0.7, 1−1e-6` are within `1e-6`.
- The `k = 0` atom equals `e^{−λ/2}` and the quantile below the atom is 0.
- The noncentral-F empirical CDF at its median is `0.5 ± 0.01` over 50,000
  sampler draws.
- The `λ = 3e5` Patnaik fallback matches a 20,000-sample empirical CDF at the
  mean within `0.02`.

The F tests caught two formula errors during development (the missing
`d1/(d1+2j)` mixture argument scale and the `c·h/d1` Patnaik factor); both are
fixed and covered by the tests.
